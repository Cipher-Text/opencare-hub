Implementation Order

The order follows the platform principle: trust before revenue.

  1. Safe to ship: security, backups, honest data
  2. Trust foundation: roles, verification, reviews
  3. Value people use: appointments, prescriptions, notifications, plans
  4. Grow and tighten: Bangla, search, analytics
  5. Money: payments and paid plans, only after the features they sell are built
  6. Later: telemedicine, mobile app, microservices
